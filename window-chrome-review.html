Findings

Shadows for elevation, borders for structure

SeverityLocationBeforeAfterWhy
Mediumwindow.tsx:19 Frame is a 2 px gray bevel, plus a 2 px gray ring, plus a 2 px sunken bevel on the body. Three nested strokes, about 6 px of mixed grays. One 2 px #fafafa border on the frame and one 2 px #fafafa divider under the title bar. The design's single white stroke reads as a crisp outline. Stacked gray bevels read as a muddy band and fight the pixel-art edges of the planets and icons.
Lowwindow.tsx:82 Hard 6px 6px black offset shadow. Remove. On a near-black desktop the shadow is a dark smear with no lift. The design has none.

Match icon stroke to text weight

SeverityLocationBeforeAfterWhy
Mediumwindow.tsx:54 Control icons at stroke={3} in dark gray inside gray bevelled boxes. Plain white icons at stroke={1.5}, no box, 40 px hit area kept, hover tints red. The design shows bare line icons beside a regular-weight title. A 3 px stroke in a bevelled box is the heaviest element on the screen and competes with the content.

Consistency with the heading language

SeverityLocationBeforeAfterWhy
Lowwindow.tsx:100 Title in the body sans font, bold. Monospace heading font, regular weight, uppercase, wide tracking. Every other heading in the hackathon windows uses the monospace recipe. The title bar is the one place still using the main site's font.

Border radius

No change. Both the design and the current window use square corners. Rounding would be the first non-pixel shape on the surface.

Verification

  • Compared native-resolution crops of the top-left corner, the title-bar controls, and the bottom-right corner against the same crops of the Figma frame.
  • Not checked: hover and pressed states of the bevelled buttons, which go away with this change.

Verdict

Needs changes

The window is a shared component, so the change lands on every hackathon window, not only sponsors. That matches the Figma file, where all windows share this chrome.
